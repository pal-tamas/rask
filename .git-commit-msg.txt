refactor(html): transform belongs to the elements that can be transformed

MDN puts `transform` on SVGGraphicsElement, not on SVGElement, and the
distinction is real rather than taxonomic: a gradient stop, a filter primitive
and a <defs> are not rendered, so there is nothing to transform. Here it sat on
SvgElement, which meant all of them carried the attribute and offered it as a
chain step and a factory parameter that could never do anything.

SvgGraphicsElement now carries it, and the elements the DOM calls graphics
derive from it: the seven geometry shapes through SvgGeometryElement, plus g,
svg, use, image, foreignObject, switch, symbol, a, text, tspan and textPath.
Everything else loses an attribute it never had in the browser.

It also moves in the output - transform is written after the presentation
attributes rather than in the middle of them, since a base writes before its
subclass. One assertion recorded the old position and records the new one.

Public API: -24 +5 per framework. Nothing in the repo set transform on a
non-graphics element, so nothing had to change at a call site.
